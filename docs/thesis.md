# THE GREAT TRANSITION

## Macro Thesis Framework

**July 2026** · desk cut 27 Jul 2026 · spine rewrite Aug 2026

> **Not financial advice.** Educational / analytical reference only.

AI is cheapening knowledge work, then labour. That makes existing public debt harder to service in real terms.

The other pressure is who used to *buy* that debt. For a long time the picture in the public mind was overpopulation: endless young workers, endless growth, endless demand for government bonds. That is the wrong map of the bond market. The people and countries that actually hold long government bonds — the surplus savers of China, Japan, East Asia, and Europe — are ageing. Fertility is already at or below replacement across that surplus world. The large older cohorts — boomers in the West, their peers in Japan and China — hold the stock of savings. They are not replaced, one-for-one, by a global pyramid of young buyers. When that surplus generation stops reinvesting in long government bonds, the bid does not automatically pass to someone else.

Seizure risk is the second shock to the same bid. In 2022 the United States and its allies froze a large share of Russia’s official reserves. Dollar clearing and access to the long bond were used as instruments of statecraft. A Treasury is a claim on a legal system that can be switched off. After that, reserve managers do not treat long G7 government paper as neutral. They can still hold bills and still use dollar *rails*. They do not have to warehouse thirty-year paper that sanctions can immobilise.

A government with a deficit must still fund it. If foreigners will not hold the long end, the remaining choices are: tax more (harder when AI is cheapening the tax base), spend less (hard in ageing democracies), sell more long paper into a shrinking bid (yields rise until someone buys), or absorb the paper with money — bills-heavy issuance, financial repression, the central bank’s balance sheet. That last path *is* more money. The official alternative is gold, which cannot be frozen in the same way. Private holders make the same substitution into gold and bitcoin.

Even if the foreign bid had stayed, cheaper intelligence would still squeeze real debt service. Even if AI were slow, a world that will not warehouse long bonds still has to print or buy gold. Either pressure is enough. AI speeds the squeeze; it is not required.

That is a 2030s claim. This desk watches whether it is still standing.

This desk owns the **2030s floor** and which A–D *path* the tape is on. It does not call whether the Jul–Dec 2026 window (`liquidity-monitor`) is in. A Q4 *signal* can print inside hawkish grind (B) without a first cut and without breaking floors. Thresholds are desk-local.

## 2. Force A — AI capability ladder

Not “inference gets cheaper” as the claim. **What intelligence can do, at what dollar, in what form.** Cost slopes are evidence that the ladder is still compounding.

### Sequence

1. **Intelligence / $ keeps compounding** for the foreseeable future — not a 2026 print.
2. **Paradigm unlocks.** 2022–25 browser LLMs. Circa 2026 the agents era (this year is *path color*, not a floor test). Next: a usefulness threshold across knowledge work → knowledge-work deflation.
3. **Embodied / OTA, slightly later, not decades.** Hardware can sit in the field looking dumb for years, then become the product through updates (Tesla-shape: 2020–25 vs 2026 software). China is already scaling robot units and capability; the US follows.
4. **Labour deflation rate goes parabolic** as specialized then general robots, plus OTA on deployed capital, hit physical work.

Knowledge work first; labour is the same mechanism moved into atoms.

### Two speeds (do not mix)

**Near term (now → 2027) — inflationary, credit-coupled.** Capex shock: memory, power, data centres, labour. **Cash** capex at the five large hyperscalers (MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META, ORCL) is growing ~**70%/yr** vs OCF ~**23%/yr** (Epoch AI). Trend **cross ~Q3 2026**. Q1 2026 still thin surplus: OCF **$158B** vs cash capex **$148B**. Oracle already crossed; Amazon is crossing. Funding is increasingly external. A financing break is near-term *deflationary for the buildout* — and, via crisis easing, a fast route to the end-state. This is **path** (A–D / stage 2), not proof that stage 1d has arrived.

**Structural (through the 2030s) — the ladder.** Observed slopes (Epoch; not forecasts): training compute ~**5×/yr**; pre-training efficiency ~**3×/yr**; inference cost at fixed capability ~**halves every ~2 months**; hardware price-performance ~**+37%/yr**. Survival of the multi-year path matters more than any single year target. Policy can lag years (Japan).

*Note:* Terafab (Tesla/SpaceX/Intel, Mar 2026, $55–119B) is a large vertical-integration capex signal — not proof of recursive manufacturing.

## 3. Force B — no duration left

De-dollarisation in one sentence: **there is no long paper left that the rest of the world will warehouse.**

- **Complementary demographics no longer obviously work.** The US-deficit / China-surplus absorber pairing is not the automatic bid it was.
- **Freeze-risk.** After 2022, G7 duration is not a neutral reserve: sovereign bonds can be seized. Official sector learned that; CB gold is the tell.
- **No alternative long end.** Japan, China, and Europe are worse as *external* duration sinks (demographics, fiscal, capital account). Japan still bids JGBs at home. What died is the **marginal foreign bid** for US (and peer) long duration.

Gold and BTC are what you buy when you cannot warehouse someone else’s 30-year.

### Official mix, still intact (evidence, not the claim)

- CB gold buying ~**850–1,045t/yr** since 2022 (≈2× pre-2022).
- CBs **bought the 2026 crash**: WGC Q1 net **244t** (+3% YoY).
- PBoC: long consecutive-month streak (e.g. June **14.9t**); use **WGC quarterly** for the structural series, national prints for color.

### 2026 complications (absorbed, not denied)

| Fact | Read |
|---|---|
| COFER USD share **up** in Q1 2026 (57.13%) | Counter-trend quarter — **noise until 4 in a row** |
| Stablecoins / T-bill rails | **Re-dollarisation** of *private* rails while official reserves diversify. Private dollars ≠ official duration. |
| Gold peak → ~$4k with CB bid intact | CB bid = **floor**; financial flows set the *path* |

Gold peak (early 2026 / 52w high) ≈ **$5,600**. Drawdown to ~$4,000 ≈ **−28%**. Structural case does not prevent 20–30% air pockets.

**Both can be true:** states diversify reserves; markets adopt tokenized dollars. Fiscal side effect: a deep bid for bills — **time bought, destination unchanged**.

## 4. Hinge — credit, the long end, fiscal gap (stages 2–3)

**Stage 2.** Corporates compete for duration (AI capex, near). The foreign official bid for the long end is impaired (Force B). Duration still has to be absorbed — bills-heavy issuance, repression / YCC-without-the-label, or a bid for the long end.

**Stage 3 — fiscal gap (inferred).** Receipts vs interest; Debt/GDP. AI deflation makes real debt harder to service over the 2030s. You also cannot export the duration. Live proxy on the desk: **US M2 rising under hawkish talk**. There is not yet a receipts / Debt/GDP series — do not invent one. Official hard-asset stock (CB gold, and any sovereign BTC) is the *other* branch, not a solvency fix: tonnes do not make Debt/GDP fall; they are a bid under gold.

Either branch still lands on stage 5.

HY OAS: calm **<3%**; watch **>4%**; **break = >5% and capex cuts**. 10Y and the Fed broad dollar are **path headwinds**, not invalidation lines.

US net liquidity (Fed − RRP − TGA) is a short-horizon complement to raw M2 — not a substitute for the global composite. It sits on **stage 2** (plumbing / path), not as the money claim.

### A — Liquidity relink (30%)

Money stays expansionary. ETF outflows exhaust; BTC re-links to liquidity. Divergence clock resets. A is the relink — not a call that 2026 hikes are off.

**Path marks:** BTC $75–95k band · gold $4,400+ · clock resets before month 18.

### B — Hawkish grind (40%) · base

Hawkish talk and threatened hikes keep the dollar firm and real rates biting. M2 still expands (fiscal math). Risk transmission stays blocked. Floors get a real test. A Q4 policy-signal can print inside B without a cut, without leaving B, and without breaking floors — grind is blocked transmission, not a veto of that signal.

**Path marks:** BTC $50–70k · gold $3,800–4,300 with $4k monthly under fire · clock runs to 15–18 months.

### C — Credit scare (20%)

Hyperscaler/AI credit accident → HY blows out → policy forced. Liquidation first, then the easing the thesis eventually wants.

**Path marks:** flush possible (weekly closes adjudicate $53k) · then policy response · hard money leads the recovery.

### D — Geo shock (10%)

**Path marks:** oil watchpoint · gold spike/correct · BTC risk-correlates until policy turns.
