## 1. Core claim

| Force | Role |
|---|---|

## 2. AI — two clocks

Hold these separately. Mixing them is how the path gets misread.

### Near term (now → 2027) — inflationary, credit-coupled

- Capex shock: memory, power, data centres, labour.
- **Cash** capex at the five large hyperscalers (MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META, ORCL) is growing ~**70%/yr** vs OCF ~**23%/yr** (Epoch AI).
- Trend **cross ~Q3 2026**. Q1 2026 still thin surplus: OCF **$158B** vs cash capex **$148B**.
- Oracle already crossed; Amazon is crossing. Funding is increasingly external, not only free cash flow.
- **Implication:** a financing break is near-term *deflationary* for the buildout — and, via crisis easing, a fast route to the thesis end-state.

### Structural (through the 2030s) — real deflation

Observed slopes (Epoch; not forecasts):

- Training compute ~**5×/yr**
- Pre-training efficiency ~**3×/yr**
- Inference cost at fixed capability ~**halves every ~2 months**
- Hardware price-performance ~**+37%/yr**

Intelligence-intensive work gets much cheaper. In a debt system that is deflationary → historically resolved by **more money**. Policy can lag years (Japan). Survival of the multi-year path matters more than any single year target.

*Note:* Terafab (Tesla/SpaceX/Intel, Mar 2026, $55–119B) is a large vertical-integration capex signal — not proof of recursive manufacturing.

- Prior tolerance 6–12 months of divergence is **used up**.
- **Clock → 18 months = transmission assumption wrong** (Section 6), whatever the narrative.

US net liquidity (Fed − RRP − TGA) is a short-horizon complement to raw M2 — not a substitute for the global composite.

## 8. Scenarios — H2 2026 → 2027

Axis: **monetary regime**. Credit and geopolitics are modifiers.

| ID | Name | p | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Liquidity relink** | 30% | Money keeps growing; transmission heals |
| **B** | **Hawkish grind** | 40% | **Base** — money grows; transmission stays blocked |
| **C** | **Credit scare** | 20% | AI/credit break → forced easing |
| **D** | **Geo shock** | 10% | Supply shock → stagflation path |

### A — Liquidity relink (30%)

Money stays expansionary. ETF outflows exhaust; BTC re-links to liquidity. Divergence clock resets. A is the relink — not a call that 2026 hikes are off.

**Path marks:** BTC $75–95k band · gold $4,400+ · clock resets before month 18.

### B — Hawkish grind (40%) · base

Hawkish talk and threatened hikes keep the dollar firm and real rates biting. M2 still expands (fiscal math). Risk transmission stays blocked. Floors get a real test. A Q4 policy-signal can print inside B without a cut, without leaving B, and without breaking floors — grind is blocked transmission, not a veto of that signal.

**Path marks:** BTC $50–70k · gold $3,800–4,300 with $4k monthly under fire · clock runs to 15–18 months.

### C — Credit scare (20%)

Hyperscaler/AI credit accident → HY blows out → policy forced. Liquidation first, then the easing the thesis eventually wants.

**Path marks:** flush possible (weekly closes adjudicate $53k) · then policy response · hard money leads the recovery.

### D — Geo shock (10%)

MoU failure or major supply shock (e.g. Hormuz-style). Oil >$120; inflation re-accelerates; Fed hawkish into scarcity. Ugly for risk; eventually supportive for hard money.

**Path marks:** oil watchpoint · gold spike/correct · BTC risk-correlates until policy turns.
