# Thesis v6 — Internal Changelog (not rendered on the dashboard)

Removed from the public thesis document by owner decision (12 Jul 2026); preserved here as provenance. Versioning (v5/v6) is internal only.

v6 (July 2026) was a structural rewrite, not a refresh. Three forcing events drove it:

- **The v5 scenario tree failed.** All four 2026 branches were built around one axis — the Gulf conflict — with the Fed as a background parameter. Reality inverted this: the geopolitical bull case resolved (Islamabad MoU, June 2026; oil back to ~$70) while an unmodeled monetary bear case fired (the Warsh Fed's hawkish institutional reset). Hard money took the bear outcome during the geopolitical resolution. The v6 tree is built around the monetary regime first.
- **The BTC invalidation line was mislabeled.** v5 called $52,000 the "ETF aggregate cost basis." It is not — the actual ETF aggregate cost basis is ~$83,800, a level BTC broke months ago *without* the structural demand collapse the trigger predicted. The metric was wrong, not the world. $53,000 is the network-wide **aggregate realized price** (average cost of all circulating supply) — the defensible structural-demand floor. The line is relabeled and re-committed at $53,000, refreshed quarterly. This is a correction of a factual error, documented so it cannot be mistaken for goalpost-moving.
- **Portfolio content removed.** The thesis document now tracks the thesis only. No positions, no tickers, no allocation tables. Equity-specific triggers are gone; the two that were genuinely thesis-level (Taiwan, AI financing) survive as macro triggers.
